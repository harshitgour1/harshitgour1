### 🏛️ Astitva & NyaySetu — Forest Governance AI

* SIH 2025 · Ministry of Tribal Affairs

Transforming India's Forest Rights Act implementation with AI — digitizing tribal land claims, spatial mapping, and automating governance workflows end-to-end.

**Features:**
- 👥 Role-based access → FRC, DLC, SDLC
- 📄 OCR + NER pipeline → digitizes legal tribal documents into FRA Atlas DB
- 🗺️ WebGIS Integration → geospatial decision support system
- 📊 Analytics Dashboard → policy-level insights for officials
- 🏛️ Dual platform — ASTITVA (officials) + NyaySetu (communities)
- ⚡ Significantly reduced claim processing time

*Bridging justice and technology for tribal India.*
